What We Do

Our Verticals

Eight specialist practices, one accountable partner.

01

Marine Consultant

Technical, commercial and regulatory consultancy for ship owners, ports, charterers and offshore operators, including vessel inspections, feasibility studies, flag compliance and due diligence.

02

Ship Building Consultant

End-to-end support from design finalization to yard selection, cost optimization and delivery, aligned with the Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047 and the India–Foreign Shipbuilding Framework to promote indigenous, green hulls.

03

Import Export & Logistics Consultant

Global EXIM documentation, customs clearance, freight forwarding (sea, air and road) and warehousing, with a focus on reducing dwell time and logistics costs for maritime cargo.

04

Marine Project Management Consultant

PMC services for shipyards, dry-docks, port infrastructure and retrofits. We manage budgets, timelines, vendors and quality with naval architect precision.

05

Marine Operations Consultant

Fleet optimization through chartering strategy, fuel efficiency, route planning, routine audits, vetting and SIRE compliance to maximize uptime and minimize OPEX.

06

Marine Human Resourcing Consultant

Compliant manning solutions with certified officers, engineers, crew and shore leadership, including training, payroll and retention for Indian and international fleets.

07

Marine Security Consultant

In light of worldwide and Strait of Hormuz attacks on vessels, we provide ISPS compliance, voyage risk assessment, anti-piracy protocols, armed guard liaison and crew security training.

08

Marine System Integrators

We act as system integrators between governments and companies to innovate and execute new maritime projects.
